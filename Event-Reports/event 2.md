# Event Report 2 – Education Awareness Camp

## Event Name

Education Awareness Camp

## Date

30 August 2026

## Location

Chennai, Tamil Nadu

## Objective

The main objective of the event was to create awareness about the importance of education and encourage students to continue their learning.

## Participants

- NGO volunteers
- Students
- Parents
- Community members

## Activities Conducted

1. Volunteers introduced the purpose of the awareness camp.
2. The importance of education was discussed.
3. Students were given guidance about learning resources.
4. Volunteers discussed good study habits.
5. Questions from students and parents were answered.

## Outcome

The event helped students and parents understand the importance of education and available learning opportunities.

## Challenges

- Limited time for interaction
- Different learning needs among participants
- Limited learning resources

## Conclusion

The Education Awareness Camp provided useful information to students and community members. The NGO plans to conduct more educational awareness activities.
