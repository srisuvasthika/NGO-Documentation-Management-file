📋 Event Details

Important information about the activity.

🏢 Organization: HopeBridge Foundation

📅 Event Name: Health Awareness Camp

📆 Date: 20 August 2026

📍 Location: Community Centre, Chennai

👥 Participants: Volunteers and community members

📸 Event Moment

🩺 About the Event

HopeBridge Foundation organized a Health Awareness Camp to encourage community members to understand the importance of healthy living, personal hygiene and preventive care.

Volunteers shared useful information about healthy habits, cleanliness, nutrition and the importance of seeking professional healthcare when necessary.

✨ Event Highlights

Key activities conducted during the event.

🩺

Health Awareness

Participants learned about healthy habits and preventive care.

🧼

Hygiene Education

Volunteers discussed the importance of personal hygiene.

🥗

Healthy Lifestyle

Participants received guidance about nutrition and healthy living.

🎯 Event Outcomes

The positive results of the activity.

❤️ Increased awareness about healthy lifestyle choices.
🧼 Improved understanding of personal hygiene.
🥗 Promoted awareness about balanced nutrition.
🤝 Encouraged community participation in health activities.

“Healthy communities begin with informed and caring people.”
